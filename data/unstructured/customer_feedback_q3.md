## 2. Product-by-Product Customer Sentiment Analysis

### 2.1 AeroSound Pro Wireless ANC Headphones (SKU: SKU-AUD-001)
- **Average Rating:** 4.4 / 5.0 (310 reviews)
- **Top Positives:**
  - Active Noise Cancellation is widely rated as comparable to $350+ premium competitors.
  - 35-hour battery life with ANC on is confirmed by 92% of reviewers.
  - Comfortable for long flights and work calls.
- **Top Customer Complaints:**
  - **Ear Cushion Wear:** 14 verified customers reported synthetic memory foam cushions beginning to flake after 3-4 months of intense gym workouts. (Support advice: remotes should remind users that sweat causes polyurethane deterioration and they are IPX4 rated, not designed for sauna/excessive sweat).
  - **Multipoint Bluetooth Switching:** Some users noted a 3-second delay when audio switches between iPhone and MacBook.

### 2.2 PulseFit Smart Tracker Band (SKU: SKU-SMT-004)
- **Average Rating:** 4.1 / 5.0 (420 reviews)
- **Top Positives:**
  - Lightweight, unobtrusive form factor.
  - Battery lasts full two weeks without recharging.
  - Accurate step counter and bright AMOLED screen under direct sunlight.
- **Top Customer Complaints:**
  - Sleep tracking algorithm inaccuracies in earlier firmware v1.2 (resolved in v1.2.4).
  - Silicone strap latch occasionally pops loose during vigorous racket sports.

### 2.3 UltraCharge 65W GaN Fast Charger (SKU: SKU-PWR-007)
- **Average Rating:** 4.7 / 5.0 (280 reviews)
- **Top Positives:**
  - Extremely compact size; replaces bulky 60W laptop brick and phone chargers.
  - Powers MacBook Pro and iPhone 16 simultaneously at rapid speed.
- **Top Customer Complaints:**
  - Unit gets warm to the touch (around 55°C) during continuous 65W laptop draw. (Normal thermal behavior within the 75°C safety ceiling).

### 2.4 Ergonomic Mechanical Keyboard Pro (SKU: SKU-KEY-002)
- **Average Rating:** 4.6 / 5.0 (195 reviews)
- **Top Positives:**
  - Gateron hot-swappable switches, smooth keystroke acoustics.
  - Split ergonomic tilt reduced wrist strain for software engineers.
- **Top Customer Complaints:**
