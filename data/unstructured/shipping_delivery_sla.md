## 1. Fulfillment & Delivery Speed Options

Nexus Logistics operates three primary fulfillment tiers:

| Shipping Method | Fulfillment Window | Transit Time (Business Days) | Base Rate | Free Shipping Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Ground** | 24 Hours | 3 - 5 Business Days | $5.99 | Free on orders over $50.00 |
| **Expedited Air** | 12 Hours | 2 Business Days | $14.99 | Free for Gold & VIP Tiers |
| **Same-Day Metro** | Cutoff 11:00 AM | Same Day by 9:00 PM | $22.50 | Exclusive to VIP in select metro areas |

---

## 2. Order Tracking & Carrier Handoff Rules

1. Tracking numbers are generated upon packing and become live within 6 hours after carrier scan.
2. If a package shows "Label Created" for more than 48 business hours without carrier physical movement, the order is flagged as **Delayed Fulfillment**.

---

## 3. Damaged or Lost Shipments Policy

### 3.1 Damaged in Transit
- Customers must submit photographs of outer box packaging and damaged merchandise within **7 business days** of carrier delivery mark.
- Nexus Logistics initiates an immediate courtesy reshipment without waiting for the carrier claim conclusion.

### 3.2 Lost in Transit (Stolen / Misdelivered)
- A package is officially declared lost if no tracking updates occur for:
  - Domestic Shipments: 7 consecutive calendar days past the estimated delivery date.
  - International Shipments: 14 consecutive calendar days past the estimated delivery date.
- Once declared lost, customer can opt for a 100% store credit with a 10% bonus or an immediate duplicate dispatch.
