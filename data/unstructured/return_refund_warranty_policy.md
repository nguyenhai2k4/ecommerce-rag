# Global Retail & E-Commerce Return, Refund & Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RET-2026-V3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 15, 2026  
**Scope:** All retail, online, and enterprise orders processed through Nexus Retail platform.

---

## 1. Return Windows and Customer Tier Eligibility

Nexus Retail recognizes customer loyalty tiers and provides distinct return windows accordingly:

| Customer Tier | Return Window (Calendar Days from Delivery) | Restocking Fee | Return Shipping Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Tier** | 30 Days | $5.00 for open box items | Customer responsibility ($6.99 flat rate label) |
| **Gold Tier** | 45 Days | Waived | Free prepaid return shipping label |
| **VIP Tier** | 60 Days | Waived | Free prepaid return shipping label + Express pickup |

*Note: The return window begins on the exact delivery confirmation date recorded in the shipping carrier tracking system, not the order placement date.*

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a full monetary refund to the original payment method:
1. Items must be returned with all original accessories, documentation, cords, and original packaging.
2. The item must not exhibit signs of user-induced physical damage, water ingress, or unauthorized modification.
3. For Standard Tier customers, if the item is opened but undamaged, a $5.00 restocking fee will be deducted from the refund balance.
4. Promotional bundles must be returned in their entirety; partial bundle returns will be credited at the discounted bundle differential.

---

## 3. Product-Specific Exceptions & Hygiene Restrictions

Due to health, sanitary, and safety regulations, special return conditions apply to the following categories:

### 3.1 In-Ear Audio Gear & Earphones (e.g., AeroBuds, PulseIn-Ear)
- **Hygiene Restriction:** In-ear audio devices can only be returned within **14 calendar days** if the retail box security seal is unbroken and unopened.
- **Exception:** If the in-ear unit is verified defective out-of-the-box (DOA) or under hardware warranty, the hygiene restriction is waived and a replacement or full refund is provided.

### 3.2 Over-Ear & On-Ear Headphones (e.g., AeroSound Pro SKU-AUD-001)
- Standard tier return window (30/45/60 days per tier) applies, provided ear cushions and headband show no physical tears or fluid damage.

### 3.3 Power Chargers & Batteries (e.g., UltraCharge 65W GaN Charger)
- Must include the original safety manual and rating label intact. Damaged prongs due to improper electrical outlets are excluded.

---

## 4. Limited Hardware Warranty Terms

All electronic hardware sold by Nexus Retail includes a manufacturer's limited hardware warranty:

1. **Standard Hardware Warranty Period:** 12 months (365 days) from purchase date, unless specified otherwise in product specifications.
2. **Coverage:**
   - Internal component failure, driver distortion, battery failing to hold >60% charge within warranty period, Bluetooth chipset malfunction.
3. **Exclusions:**
   - Cosmetic wear and tear, drops, water submersion (exceeding product IP rating), and third-party charger voltage surges.
4. **Resolution Path:**
   - Under 30 days: Immediate brand-new replacement or full refund.
   - 31 to 365 days: Free repair or certified refurbished exchange.
